How it works

One app for both sides of the road.

Whether you have goods to move or a vehicle with space to fill, TeleTransit puts you in touch with the right people, from the big cities to the smaller towns.

Senders

I have goods to move

  1. Post your transport needCargo type, size, pickup and delivery points, and your dates.
  2. Compare vehicles and routesSee who is going your way, with what capacity and when. Keep a fixed route or allow nearby stops.
  3. Request a connectionWhen the provider accepts, you get each other's numbers and agree the details.

Posting a need is free. Providers who can carry it can reach you directly.

Transport providers

I have space in my vehicle

  1. List your vehicle and routeWhere you're going, when, and how much space you have.
  2. Find customers across TanzaniaBrowse senders' needs and fill the empty space on trips you're already making.
  3. Connect and agree the priceOpen a customer's number and deal directly. No middleman takes a cut of your fare.

Listing your vehicles is free, up to 8 at a time. You pay only to open a customer's number: try it free for 7 days, then packages start from TZS 1,000.
